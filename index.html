Deforestation

Deforestation in the Amazon threatens biodiversity and accelerates climate change, impacting millions of people and species across Latin America.

Malaria & Health

Malaria remains a critical public health challenge in the Amazon, driven by environmental changes that create ideal breeding conditions for disease vectors.

Wildfires

Devastating wildfires release billions of tons of CO₂, degrade ecosystems, and endanger local communities and indigenous peoples across the Amazon.

Illegal Mining

Illegal gold mining contaminates watersheds with mercury, destroys rainforest habitats, and poses severe risks to One Health in affected communities.

Integrating climate and health research to empower communities and ecosystems across Latin America with sustainable, data-driven solutions.
